Lunch Doctor

午餐醫生

Patients come to your clinic feeling unwell. Look at their lunch, find the missing food group, and give them the right food.

How to play 怎麼玩

  1. The picker chooses a doctor and a patient.
    隨機抽出一位醫生和一位病人。
  2. Patient: read the speech bubble aloud.
    病人：大聲唸出對話框。
  3. Doctor: say “This lunch has no ___.” Teacher taps ✓.
    醫生：說出缺少什麼，老師按 ✓。
  4. Doctor: drag the missing food to the tray and say “Add ___.”
    醫生：把食物拖到空格，說出句子。
Names are saved for each class.

Each turn, the picker chooses a doctor and a patient. Everyone is the doctor once and earns their own points. 每位學生當一次醫生，自己得分。
